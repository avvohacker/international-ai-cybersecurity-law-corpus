## Discovery versus verification

Search snippets were used to locate candidates and, in some cases, to read an official page's key statements. They were not treated as a substitute for reading a document. Records distinguish:

- full text stored and checked;
- official page/excerpt read;
- official source merely located.

Secondary literature is used only in the atlas's analytical frame (section 02) and is cited there. It is not used to establish the legal status, parties or dates of any instrument; those remain based on primary sources.

The candidate register contains 22 entries: 8 included (ISO/IEC 23053, the CCW GGE on LAWS, the Paris AI Action Summit Statement, China's Global AI Governance Initiative, the UK–US AI Safety MOU, the US–EU TTC AI-safety dialogue commitment, the WAICO Establishment Agreement and the Pax Silica Declaration), 7 pending and 7 excluded. Pending items include the International AI Safety Report, Council of Europe Recommendation CM/Rec(2020)1, ITU-T Y.3172, IEEE 7000 and CEN-CENELEC JTC 21.

## Negative and provisional results

- No adopted treaty specifically dedicated to AI other than CETS 225 and the WAICO Establishment Agreement was located.
- No public WAICO depositary register or notification was located; China's national approval is not treated as a deposited ratification, and the 38th founding member remains unnamed in the official record.
- No adopted UN framework convention specific to AI was located.
- No standalone US–EU AI-safety MOU, administrative arrangement or terms of reference was located; the April 2024 commitment is a political Dialogue inside the TTC joint statement, not a binding agreement.
- China's Global AI Governance Initiative is a unilateral government proposal, not an agreed international instrument; no formal adherence list was found, and the record does not attribute it to other States.
- WTO JSI e-commerce, CPTPP, USMCA and RCEP were recorded as excluded **provisionally**, because a full-text AI keyword check was not performed. They are not claims that those agreements contain no AI reference.
- Category 6 now contains one negotiation record (CCW GGE on LAWS); no adopted draft protocol or treaty text exists.
