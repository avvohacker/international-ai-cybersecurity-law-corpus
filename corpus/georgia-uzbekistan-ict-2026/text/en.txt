AGREEMENT
BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF GEORGIA AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF UZBEKISTAN ON COOPERATION IN THE FIELDS OF INFORMATION AND COMMUNICATION TECHNOLOGIES, DIGITALIZATION, INNOVATIONS AND POST

The Government of GEORGIA and the Government of the REPUBLIC OF UZBEKISTAN (hereinafter referred to as the “Parties” and individually as a “Party”),

STRIVING to develop and strengthen mutually beneficial cooperation in the field of Information and Communication Technologies (hereinafter — “ICTs”), Digitalization, Innovations and Post within the framework of the social and economic development of both countries;

RECOGNIZING the mutual interest in expanding existing cooperation, opportunities, and prospects between Georgia and the Republic of Uzbekistan in the areas of ICTs, 5G, Internet of Things (IoT), cloud services, artificial intelligence, postal services, digitalization;

UNDERSTANDING the necessity of fully utilizing the existing potential and opportunities to strengthen economic relations in the ICTs, Digitalization, Innovations and Postal fields;

BEING GUIDED by the common goals and interests of providing effective and sustainable services to the public in both countries;

ACKNOWLEDGING the importance of broadband infrastructure for digitalization and the need to deepen cooperation in this regard;

RECOGNIZING the significance of expanding mutually beneficial cooperation in the ICTs, Digitalization, Innovations and Postal fields and the role of new technological progress and economic relations in improving the quality of life;

HAVE AGREED as follows:

ARTICLE 1
Objectives of Cooperation

The Parties shall base their relations on mutual benefit. They undertake to promote the development and deepening of cooperation in the areas of ICTs, postal services, digitalization, and innovations in accordance with their respective national legislation, international legal norms and principles, and the provisions of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 2
Areas of Cooperation

The key areas of cooperation between the Parties, without limiting the rights of either Party to undertake further initiatives, shall include:

- Exchange of information on policies and programs/projects in the fields of ICTs, postal services, digitalization, and innovations, and facilitation of investments in these areas;
- Encourage the cooperation between the Parties in extending the exchange of digital traffic in order to connect Europe and Asia through the territories of the Parties;
- Cooperate for development of broadband telecommunication infrastructure;
- Support for the development of data processing centers and cloud computing infrastructure;
- Promotion of entrepreneurship in ICT software development;
- Support identification and development of joint scientific research and experimental design projects;
- Support for the development of the Innovations and IT ecosystem;
- Utilization of information technologies to improve the functioning of digital public services included but not limited to:
  - Introduction of telemedicine, including remote medical consultations and diagnostics through digital technologies;
  - Strengthening the digital transformation of educational institutions through the integration of digital technologies and further advancement of online and distance education opportunities;
  - Introduction and widespread use of electronic signature and digital identification technologies.
- Encouragement of cooperation between relevant organizations, universities, and businesses in the fields of information technology, postal services, artificial intelligence, and digitalization;
- Organization of joint seminars, conferences, forums, and exhibitions in the ICTs, Digitalization, Innovations and Postal fields;
- Facilitation of the development of the postal field through increased volume of international postal shipments via Georgia and Uzbekistan;
- Mutual support in international organizations (such as the Universal Postal Union and the International Telecommunication Union) where national interests align;
- Organization of expert visits and study tours to exchange experience.

The Parties also agree to support projects in areas such as the development of high-speed communication networks, the application of artificial intelligence, the advancement of technologies, and the implementation of smart city solutions — provided that these activities do not contradict their commitments under other international treaties.

ARTICLE 3
Competent Authorities

The competent authorities responsible for the implementation of this Agreement shall be:

- On behalf of the Government of Georgia — the Ministry of Economy and Sustainable Development of Georgia;
- On behalf of the Government of the Republic of Uzbekistan — the Ministry of Digital Technologies of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

The Parties shall notify each other through diplomatic channels of any changes to the competent authorities.

ARTICLE 4
Intellectual Property Rights

Issues relating to the distribution of any intellectual property rights created during or as a result of the implementation of this Agreement shall be governed by separate contracts concluded for each specific area of cooperation under this Agreement.

Each Party shall retain ownership of any intellectual property rights it created independently prior to the commencement of joint activities or independently of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 5
Confidentiality

The Parties shall take necessary measures, in accordance with their respective national legislation, to protect any confidential information provided by one Party to the other during their joint activities and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the originating Party. The provisions of this Article shall remain in effect even after the termination of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 6
Settlement of Disputes

Any disputes or disagreements arising between the Parties concerning the interpretation and/or application of the provisions of this Agreement shall be resolved peacefully through negotiations and/or consultations between the Parties.

ARTICLE 7
Language of Correspondence

All correspondence between the Parties regarding the implementation of this Agreement shall be conducted in the English language.

ARTICLE 8
Amendments and Additions

Amendments and additions may be made to this Agreement by mutual consent of the Parties, which shall constitute an integral part of the Agreement and shall be formalized through separate documents.

ARTICLE 9
Relationship with other international treaties

The provisions of this Agreement shall not affect the rights and obligations of the Parties arising from other international treaties to which they are parties.

ARTICLE 10
Final Provisions

This Agreement shall enter into force on the date of its signature and shall remain in effect until three months after the date on which one Party receives, through diplomatic channels of its intention to terminate the Agreement.

The Agreement shall be concluded for a period of four (4) years and shall be automatically extended for successive four (4) year periods.

Unless otherwise agreed by the Parties, the termination of this Agreement shall not affect the implementation of joint programs and projects initiated during its term and not completed by the date of termination.

With the entry into force of this Agreement, the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Georgia and the Government of the Republic of Uzbekistan on cooperation in the field of telecommunications concluded on September 4, 1995 shall cease to be effective.

The duly authorized representatives of the Parties have signed this Agreement.

DONE at Tbilisi, on 3 July 2026, in two original copies, each in Georgian, Uzbek, and English languages, all texts being equally authentic. In case of divergence in interpretation of the provisions of this Agreement, the English text shall prevail.

ON BEHALF OF THE GOVERNMENT OF GEORGIA

ON BEHALF OF THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF UZBEKISTAN
